About Subbie Calculator.

A simple Australian comparison tool for workers, subcontractors and employers.

Subbie Calculator helps compare TFN employee pay with PTY/ABN subcontractor rates and helps businesses estimate an employee’s true cost and charge-out rate.

The calculator lets users enter their actual ordinary rate, overtime rate and double-time rate rather than assuming every workplace uses the same overtime multipliers. It also allows for super, annual leave, public holidays, casual loading, leave loading, payroll tax, workers compensation and business overheads.

The results are general estimates only and are intended to make rate comparisons easier to understand.
