Why these rates are different

A TFN employee rate, PTY/ABN subcontractor rate and employer charge-out rate measure different things. These calculators help put super, paid leave, public holidays, overtime and business on-costs into the same comparison.

For permanent employees, paid public holidays are treated as paid non-working time. To avoid double-counting wages, the calculator leaves the annual wage calculation unchanged and reduces PTY/ABN or employer billable hours by the public-holiday hours instead.
