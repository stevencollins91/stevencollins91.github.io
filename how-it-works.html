How the calculators work.

Subbie Calculator compares employee pay, subcontractor rates and employer costs using the assumptions you enter.

TFN → PTY/ABN

Enter ordinary hours and base rate, then enter the actual overtime hourly rate and double-time hourly rate you are paid. The calculator adds allowances, super, annual leave, public holidays, optional leave loading, payroll tax and PTY/ABN overhead to estimate the contractor rate needed.

Weekly gross wages

You can enter a weekly gross wage instead of a base hourly rate. The calculator subtracts the allowances and the overtime pay you entered, then estimates the ordinary base rate from the remaining ordinary-hours pay.

PTY/ABN → TFN

Enter the contractor ordinary and overtime invoice rates. For the employee comparison you can separately enter the actual TFN overtime and double-time hourly rates. The calculator then works backwards to estimate the equivalent TFN base rate.

Employer rate

The employer calculator uses the actual employee ordinary, overtime and double-time rates plus super, leave, public holidays, workers compensation, payroll tax, other overheads and the selected profit margin to estimate a charge-out rate.

Public holidays

For permanent employees, public holidays are treated as paid non-working time. The calculator reduces annual billable hours rather than adding the same wage twice.

Leave loading and payroll tax

Leave loading defaults to 0% and can be changed by the user. Payroll tax is also entered manually because rates and thresholds vary by state and territory.

Tax estimates

Resident mode uses the displayed Australian resident tax assumptions and a rough Medicare levy. Working holiday maker mode uses the WHM rates shown in the calculator. These are estimates, not payroll or tax-return calculations.
